Good to know

  • Codes unlock after your first paid order.
  • Your friend enters your code at checkout — it can't be applied after the order is placed.
  • A referral counts once their payment clears, not when the order is placed.
  • It's three different people — one person using your code twice still counts once.
  • On the third, you get one free 30 ml of the cheapest fragrance in your next basket, added automatically.
  • You can't use your own code.
