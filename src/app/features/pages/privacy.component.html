What we collect

When you create an account, place an order or contact us we collect your name, email address, phone number, delivery/billing address, order contents and order notes. Why we use it

We use your information to process and deliver orders, confirm payments, provide customer support, manage returns, prevent fraud, and keep records required by law. With your consent, we may send occasional product updates; you can opt out at any time.

Who we share it with

We share only what is necessary with service providers that help us run the shop: our hosting and database provider, our email provider, our payment provider and our courier. They may process information outside South Africa; we use providers with appropriate safeguards. We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies and local storage

We use browser storage to keep your cart and saved fragrances, and to keep you signed in. We do not use advertising cookies. If we add analytics later, we will ask for consent where required.

How long we keep it

We keep order and payment records for as long as required for tax, accounting and dispute purposes. Account details are kept until you ask us to delete them, subject to those legal record-keeping obligations.
